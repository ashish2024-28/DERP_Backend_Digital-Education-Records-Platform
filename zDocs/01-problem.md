# 01 — Problem Statement

## 1. Background

The idea behind DERP originated from personal experience with academic record management.

The first prototype was created during a hackathon using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The prototype solved only a small part of the problem. During development, several larger problems became visible.

## 2. Student Problem

A student can accumulate many educational records:

```text
Certificates
Workshops
Internships
Achievements
Semester Results
Projects
Training
Participation
Academic Documents
```

These records are often stored separately.

For example:

```text
Google Drive
WhatsApp
Email
Phone Storage
College ERP
Physical Documents
Different Websites
```

This makes it difficult to answer a simple question:

> "What have I achieved during my degree?"

## 3. Resume Problem

When preparing a resume, students may remember their achievements but struggle to collect them.

The process becomes:

```text
Need Resume
    ↓
Search certificates
    ↓
Search email
    ↓
Search WhatsApp
    ↓
Search gallery
    ↓
Search college portal
    ↓
Try to remember achievements
    ↓
Manually organize everything
```

DERP aims to reduce this friction.

## 4. Attendance Problem

College attendance systems can create operational problems.

Examples include:

* Slow response
* ERP downtime
* Crashes
* Repeated submission
* Delayed attendance entry
* Manual work
* Poor visibility
* Data submission failures

This affects both students and faculty.

## 5. Faculty Problem

Faculty members may need to:

```text
Open ERP
    ↓
Select subject
    ↓
Select class
    ↓
Select date
    ↓
Mark attendance
    ↓
Submit
    ↓
Wait
    ↓
Verify
```

If submission fails, the process may need to be repeated.

A better system should minimize unnecessary work.

## 6. Administrative Problem

Administrators need to manage:

* Students
* Faculty
* Departments
* Courses
* Subjects
* Attendance
* Results
* Certificates
* Fees

These areas can become difficult to manage when information is fragmented.

## 7. Long-Term Problem

The problem is therefore larger than:

> "Build a certificate management website."

The actual problem is:

> Educational information is fragmented across different systems and workflows, creating unnecessary manual work for students, faculty, and administrators.

## 8. Problem Statement

DERP aims to provide a centralized platform for managing educational records and academic workflows while reducing duplicate work and improving accessibility.

## 9. Key Goals

* Centralize educational records.
* Reduce repetitive data entry.
* Improve attendance workflows.
* Organize certificates and achievements.
* Make student records easier to access.
* Support administrative workflows.
* Provide secure role-based access.
* Create a foundation for future institutional systems.

## 10. Non-Goals

The initial system will not attempt to replace every function of a university ERP.

Examples of possible non-goals for the MVP:

* Complete university accounting
* Payroll
* Library management
* Hostel management
* Transport management
* Examination board integration

These can be evaluated later.
